## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Cook the brown rice. Refrigerate. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup, Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls)
- [ ] Cook the green lentils (about 1 cup dry makes 3 cups cooked): simmer in plenty of water for 20–25 mins until tender, then drain. Refrigerate. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup)
- [ ] Dice the onion. Refrigerate. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup)
- [ ] Halve the cherry tomatoes. Refrigerate. (Caprese Pesto Gnocchi)
- [ ] Shred the carrots and finely dice the bell pepper. Refrigerate together. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
- [ ] Whisk the tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil in a jar. Refrigerate. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
- [ ] Wash and separate the lettuce leaves, pat dry, and refrigerate wrapped in a paper towel. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
- [ ] Halve the cherry tomatoes and finely dice the onion. Refrigerate. (Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls)
- [ ] Pat the chickpeas dry. Toss with olive oil, lemon zest, and salt; roast at 400°F for 25–30 mins, shaking halfway, until crisp. Refrigerate. (Warm Chickpea & Asparagus Salad)
