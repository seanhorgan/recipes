## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Heat the oven to 400°F. Rinse and drain 2 cans chickpeas and pat dry. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, the zest of 1 lemon, and 1/2 tsp salt; roast for 25–30 mins, shaking halfway, until crisp. Refrigerate. (Warm Chickpea & Asparagus Salad)
- [ ] Cook the brown rice for both dinners in one pot: bring 1 1/3 cups brown rice and 3 cups water to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 45 mins (makes 4 cups cooked: 2 cups for the soup, 2 cups for the bowls). Refrigerate. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup, Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls)
- [ ] Cook the green lentils: rinse 1 cup dry green lentils, cover with 3 cups water, and simmer 20–25 mins until tender (makes about 3 cups cooked). Drain. Refrigerate. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup)
- [ ] Dice 1 1/2 yellow onions: 1 onion for the soup and 1/2 onion, finely diced, for the bowls. Refrigerate in separate containers. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup, Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls)
- [ ] Halve 1 pint plus 1 cup cherry tomatoes: 1 pint for the gnocchi and 1 cup for the bowls. Refrigerate in separate containers. (Caprese Pesto Gnocchi, Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls)
- [ ] Shred 2 carrots and finely dice 1 bell pepper. Refrigerate together. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
- [ ] Make the sauce: grate 1 tbsp fresh ginger and mince 2 cloves garlic. Whisk them in a jar with 3 tbsp tamari, 1 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, and 1 tsp toasted sesame oil. Refrigerate. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
- [ ] Separate the leaves from 2 heads butter lettuce, wash, pat dry, and refrigerate wrapped in a paper towel. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
- [ ] Chop 1/2 cup cashews. Store at room temperature. (Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps)
