## Monday: [Smoky Black Bean & Sweet Potato Tacos](../../recipes/smoky-black-bean-sweet-potato-tacos.md)
**For 3:** 3 cups roasted sweet potatoes (from Sunday), 1 can (15 oz) black beans, 1 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp salt, 1 tsp olive oil, 9 corn tortillas, 2 avocados, 1/4 lime, 1/4 cup pickled red onions, 3/4 cup shredded cheese.
1. Rinse and drain the black beans.
2. Heat 1 tsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sweet potatoes, beans, 1 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp salt, and 2 tbsp water. Cook 5–6 mins, stirring now and then, until hot. Move to a bowl and cover.
3. Wipe out the skillet and set it over medium-high heat. Warm the tortillas 30–45 seconds per side, a few at a time, until lightly charred. Stack them in a clean kitchen towel to stay warm.
4. Mash the 2 avocados in a bowl with the juice of 1/4 lime and a pinch of salt.
5. Fill each tortilla with about 1/3 cup of the filling, a spoonful of avocado, a few pickled onions, and a sprinkle of cheese. 3 tacos each.
Kid boost: the shredded cheese adds protein.

## Tuesday: [Hearty Shakshuka with Feta](../../recipes/hearty-shakshuka-feta.md)
**For 3:** 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 diced bell peppers (from Sunday), 1 jar (23.5 oz) marinara sauce, 1 tsp cumin, 6 eggs, 4 oz feta (half the block), 6 slices GF bread.
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a 12-inch skillet that has a lid, over medium heat. Cook the peppers 5 mins until soft.
2. Stir in the marinara and 1 tsp cumin. Simmer 5 mins.
3. Use a spoon to make 6 wells in the sauce. Crack an egg into each well.
4. Turn the heat to medium-low, cover, and cook 6–8 mins, until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft. Cook 2 mins more for firm yolks.
5. Meanwhile, toast the 6 slices of GF bread.
6. Crumble 4 oz feta over the top. Serve 2 eggs each with 2 slices of toast for dipping.

## Wednesday: [Greek Turkey Meatballs with Roasted Broccoli](../../recipes/greek-turkey-meatballs-roasted-broccoli.md)
**For 3:** the full recipe: 16 meatballs (rolled Sunday), 1 bag (10 oz) broccoli florets, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 tsp salt, 1 1/2 cups cooked quinoa (from Sunday), 1 jar Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce (from Sunday).
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. On the meatball sheet pan, toss the broccoli with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/4 tsp salt on the empty half.
3. Roast 16–18 mins, until the meatballs are browned and 165°F inside, and the broccoli edges are crisp.
4. While it roasts, warm the 1 1/2 cups quinoa in the microwave with 1 tbsp water, covered, for 2 mins.
5. Serve about 5 meatballs each over 1/2 cup quinoa with the broccoli, and spoon the yogurt sauce on top.

## Thursday: [Zucchini & Ricotta GF Flatbreads](../../recipes/zucchini-ricotta-gf-flatbreads.md)
**For 3:** 3 GF flatbreads, the chickpea-ricotta spread (from Sunday), sliced zucchini (from Sunday), 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional), 1 cup arugula.
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Set the 3 flatbreads on a large sheet pan.
2. Brush each flatbread with 1 tsp olive oil.
3. Spread about 2/3 cup of the chickpea-ricotta mixture over each flatbread, almost to the edges.
4. Lay the zucchini rounds over the top in a single layer. Drizzle the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over the zucchini and sprinkle with a pinch of salt and the red pepper flakes, if using (skip them on the kids' flatbreads).
5. Bake 12–14 mins, until the edges are crisp and the zucchini is tender.
6. Top each with a small handful of arugula (about 1/3 cup). Cut into wedges.

## Friday: [Crispy Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry](../../recipes/crispy-tofu-green-bean-stir-fry.md)
**For 3:** 2 blocks pressed, cubed tofu (from Sunday), 3 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tbsp avocado oil, 12 oz trimmed green beans (from Sunday), 1 1/2 cups cooked brown rice (from Sunday), the stir-fry sauce jar (from Sunday).
1. Put the tofu cubes in a large bowl, sprinkle with 3 tbsp cornstarch, and toss until coated.
2. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu in a single layer and cook 6–8 mins, turning every 2 mins, until golden and crisp on most sides. Move to a plate.
3. Add the remaining 1 tbsp avocado oil, the green beans, and 2 tbsp water to the skillet. Cover and cook 2 mins, then uncover and stir-fry 3 mins until bright green and tender-crisp.
4. Warm the 1 1/2 cups rice in the microwave with 1 tbsp water, covered, for 2 mins.
5. Shake the sauce jar, then return the tofu to the pan, pour the sauce over, and toss 1–2 mins until glossy and hot.
6. Serve over 1/2 cup rice each.
