## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Roast the cauliflower and chickpeas: heat the oven to 400°F. Cut any large cauliflower florets into bite-size pieces and pat 2 cans rinsed chickpeas dry. Toss both on 2 parchment-lined sheet pans with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 3/4 tsp salt. Roast 25 mins, stirring halfway, until browned at the edges. Cool. Refrigerate. (Crispy Chickpea & Cauliflower Tostadas)
- [ ] Spiralize 2 zucchini (or cut them into thin ribbons with a vegetable peeler). Refrigerate in a container lined with a paper towel. (Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta)
- [ ] Toast 2 tbsp pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3–4 mins, shaking often, until golden. Cool and store at room temperature. (Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta)
- [ ] Cook the quinoa: rinse 1/3 cup quinoa, bring to a boil with 2/3 cup water, cover, and simmer on low 15 mins. Rest 5 mins covered, then fluff (makes about 1 cup). (Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers)
- [ ] Make the filling: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 lb ground turkey, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper. Break into small crumbles and cook 6–7 mins until no pink remains. Off the heat, stir in the quinoa and 1 cup tomato sauce. (Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers)
- [ ] Stuff the peppers: halve 4 bell peppers lengthwise through the stem and pull out the seeds and ribs. Set them cut-side up in a 9x13-inch baking dish and fill each half with about 1/2 cup filling. Cover with foil and refrigerate. (Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers)
- [ ] Roast the squash: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 2 lb cubed butternut squash with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 30–35 mins, flipping halfway, until tender and browned at the edges. Cool. Refrigerate. (Creamy Butternut Squash & White Bean Soup)
- [ ] Roast the potatoes: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 1 lb cubed yellow potatoes and 1/2 diced yellow onion with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 25 mins, stirring halfway, until tender and golden. Cool. Refrigerate. (Spinach, Potato & Feta Frittata)
