## Sunday Prep
- [x] Roast the cauliflower and chickpeas: heat the oven to 400°F. Cut any large cauliflower florets into bite-size pieces and pat 2 cans rinsed chickpeas dry. Toss both on 2 parchment-lined sheet pans with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 3/4 tsp salt. Roast 25 mins, stirring halfway, until browned at the edges. Cool. Refrigerate. (Crispy Chickpea & Cauliflower Tostadas)
- [x] Spiralize 2 zucchini (or cut them into thin ribbons with a vegetable peeler). Refrigerate in a container lined with a paper towel. (Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta)
- [x] Toast 2 tbsp pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3–4 mins, shaking often, until golden. Cool and store at room temperature. (Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta)
- [x] Roast the potatoes: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 1 lb cubed yellow potatoes and 1/2 diced yellow onion with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 25 mins, stirring halfway, until tender and golden. Cool. Refrigerate. (Spinach, Potato & Feta Frittata)
- [x] Roast the squash: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 2 lb cubed butternut squash with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 30–35 mins, flipping halfway, until tender and browned at the edges. Cool. Refrigerate. (Creamy Butternut Squash & White Bean Soup)
