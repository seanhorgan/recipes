## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Par-roast the potatoes: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss the halved potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, the oregano, and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 20 mins until just tender. Cool. Refrigerate. (Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes)
- [ ] Cut the red onion into wedges. Refrigerate. (Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes)
- [ ] Make the spinach sauce: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat and cook the onion 6 mins until soft. Stir in the garlic, ginger, garam masala, cumin, and turmeric for 1 min. Add the crushed tomatoes and 1/2 tsp salt; simmer 5 mins. Add the spinach by handfuls until wilted. Blend smooth with 1/2 cup water. Cool. Refrigerate. (Creamy Spinach & Paneer Curry)
- [ ] Cook the brown rice. Refrigerate. (Creamy Spinach & Paneer Curry)
- [ ] Whisk the peanut sauce ingredients together until smooth. Refrigerate. (Peanut Rice Noodles with Edamame & Scrambled Egg)
- [ ] Make the Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce (for Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes)
