## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Par-roast the potatoes: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 1.5 lb halved baby potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 20 mins until just tender. Cool. Refrigerate. (Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes)
- [ ] Cut 1 red onion into wedges. Refrigerate. (Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes)
- [x] Make the spinach sauce: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat and cook 1 diced yellow onion 6 mins until soft. Stir in 3 cloves minced garlic, 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, and 1/2 tsp turmeric for 1 min. Add 1 cup crushed tomatoes and 1/2 tsp salt; simmer 5 mins. Add 10 oz spinach by handfuls until wilted. Blend smooth with 1/2 cup water. Cool. Refrigerate. (Creamy Spinach & Paneer Curry)
- [ ] Cook 1.5 cups brown rice. Refrigerate. (Creamy Spinach & Paneer Curry)
- [ ] Make the peanut sauce: whisk 1/3 cup peanut butter, 3 tbsp tamari, the juice of 1 lime, 1 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger, and 1/4 cup water until smooth. Refrigerate. (Peanut Rice Noodles with Edamame & Scrambled Egg)
- [ ] Make the Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce: 1/2 cup Greek yogurt; 1 lemon, juiced; 1 tbsp honey; 1/2 tsp garlic powder; 2 tbsp water (for Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes)
