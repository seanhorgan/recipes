# Week of November 23, 2026

## Monday: [Black Bean & Roasted Sweet Potato Power Bowls](../../recipes/black-bean-roasted-sweet-potato-power-bowls.md)
High protein: be generous with the yogurt sauce and pumpkin seeds (both add protein on top of the beans and quinoa).

## Tuesday: [Red Lentil & Coconut "Dal"](../../recipes/red-lentil-coconut-dal.md)
Serve over quinoa: on Sunday, cook a double batch (4 cups dry) so it covers Monday and Tuesday, and skip the separate "Cook the grains" step.
High protein: use 1½ cups red lentils instead of 1 (one 16 oz bag covers it). Kid boost: a dollop of Greek yogurt (buy a large tub) and a sprinkle of pumpkin seeds on top.

## Wednesday: [Red Lentil Pasta with White Beans & Spinach](../../recipes/red-lentil-pasta-white-beans-spinach.md)
Buy **chickpea** pasta (e.g. Banza), not red lentil, so it doesn't repeat Tuesday's lentils. Kid boost: grated Parmesan on top.
