## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Boil the carrots and unpeeled potatoes together until tender (about 15 mins). (Turmeric-Ginger Cod with Roasted Root Mash)
- [ ] Drain and mash with olive oil and a splash of coconut milk or broth, **keeping the potato skins in the mash** for texture and nutrients. Refrigerate. (Turmeric-Ginger Cod with Roasted Root Mash)
- [ ] Mix the ricotta with the Parmesan and **2 cans of mashed chickpeas** for a high-protein base. Refrigerate. (Zucchini & Ricotta GF Flatbreads)
- [ ] Slice the zucchini or squash into rounds. (Zucchini & Ricotta GF Flatbreads)
- [ ] Toss the cubed sweet potatoes with olive oil and salt; roast at 400°F for 20 mins until tender. Refrigerate. (Smoky Black Bean & Sweet Potato Tacos)
- [ ] Halve the radishes. Refrigerate. (Lemon Chicken & Roasted Radishes)
- [ ] Whisk the lemon juice and mustard together for the pan drizzle. (Lemon Chicken & Roasted Radishes)
