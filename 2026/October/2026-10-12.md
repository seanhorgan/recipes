## Sunday Prep
- [x] Boil the potatoes until just fork-tender. Drain and refrigerate. (Salmon & Smashed Potatoes with Green Beans)
- [ ] Trim the green beans. (Salmon & Smashed Potatoes with Green Beans)

- [ ] Cook the quinoa. (Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers)
