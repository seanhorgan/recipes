## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Drain 2 blocks (14 oz each) extra-firm tofu, wrap each block in a clean kitchen towel, and press under a heavy pan for 15 mins. Cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Refrigerate in a container lined with a paper towel. (Crispy Sesame-Ginger Tofu Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles)
- [ ] Make the sauce: grate 1 tbsp fresh ginger and mince 2 cloves garlic. Whisk them in a jar with 1/4 cup tamari, 2 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, and 1 tsp toasted sesame oil. Refrigerate. (Crispy Sesame-Ginger Tofu Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles)
- [ ] Cut 3 cups broccoli into bite-size florets and slice 1 red bell pepper into thin strips. Refrigerate together in a zip-top bag with 2 cups sugar snap peas. (Crispy Sesame-Ginger Tofu Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles)
- [ ] Dice 1 yellow onion and mince 3 cloves garlic. Refrigerate together in a small container. (Creamy White Bean & Spinach Soup)
- [ ] Finely dice 1 yellow onion, peel and finely dice 2 carrots, and mince 3 cloves garlic. (Turkey Bolognese with GF Penne)
- [ ] Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and carrots with 1/2 tsp salt and cook 7–8 mins, stirring, until soft. Add the garlic for 1 min. (Turkey Bolognese with GF Penne)
- [ ] Raise the heat to medium-high, add 1 lb ground turkey, and break it into small crumbles with a wooden spoon. Cook 6–7 mins until no pink remains. (Turkey Bolognese with GF Penne)
- [ ] Stir in 1 jar (23.5 oz) marinara sauce, 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp black pepper. Simmer uncovered on low for 15 mins, stirring now and then, until thick. Cool and refrigerate. (Turkey Bolognese with GF Penne)
- [ ] Heat the oven to 400°F. Scrub 5 large sweet potatoes, prick each 4–5 times with a fork, and set them on a foil-lined sheet pan. Roast for 45–50 mins until a knife slides in easily. Cool, then refrigerate whole. (Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Mediterranean Chickpeas)
- [ ] Make the Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze (for Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Mediterranean Chickpeas)
