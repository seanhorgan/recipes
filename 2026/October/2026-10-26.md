## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Rinse the quinoa in a fine-mesh strainer. Bring it to a boil with the 2 cups water and 1/4 tsp salt, cover, and simmer on low for 15 mins. Turn off the heat, rest 5 mins covered, fluff with a fork, cool, and refrigerate. (Roasted Cod & Lemon-Garlic Cauliflower)
- [ ] Cut the cauliflower into 1-inch florets, skipping this if you bought pre-cut florets. Store in a zip-top bag in the fridge. (Roasted Cod & Lemon-Garlic Cauliflower)
- [ ] Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Refrigerate in a small container. (Creamy White Bean & Spinach Soup)
- [ ] Snap the woody ends off the asparagus and cut the spears into 1 1/2-inch pieces. Refrigerate in a zip-top bag. (Shrimp & Asparagus Stir-Fry with Cashews)
- [ ] Heat the oven to 400°F. Scrub the sweet potatoes, prick each 4–5 times with a fork, and set them on a foil-lined sheet pan. Roast for 45–50 mins until a knife slides in easily. Cool, then refrigerate whole. (Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Mediterranean Chickpeas)
- [ ] Make the Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze (for Shrimp & Asparagus Stir-Fry with Cashews, Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Mediterranean Chickpeas)
