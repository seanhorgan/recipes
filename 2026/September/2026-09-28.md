## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate. (Seared Scallops with Warm Asparagus Salad, Roasted Cod & Lemon-Garlic Cauliflower, Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers)
- [ ] Cut the asparagus into 1-inch pieces. (Seared Scallops with Warm Asparagus Salad)
- [ ] Toss the cubed sweet potatoes with olive oil and salt; roast at 400°F for 20 mins until tender. Refrigerate. (Smoky Black Bean & Sweet Potato Tacos)
- [ ] Spiralize the zucchini. Toast the pine nuts. (Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta)

- [ ] Stuff the peppers, cover, and refrigerate. (Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers)
