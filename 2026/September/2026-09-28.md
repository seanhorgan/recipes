## Sunday Prep
- [ ] Peel and cube the sweet potatoes; toss with oil and salt and roast at 400°F for 25 mins. Refrigerate. (Crispy Turmeric Salmon & Sweet Potato "Steaks") — Ali
- [ ] Toss the chickpeas with olive oil, lemon zest, and salt; roast at 400°F until crisp. Refrigerate. (Warm Chickpea & Asparagus Salad)
- [ ] Press the tofu and cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Refrigerate. (Crispy Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry)
- [ ] Whisk the tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil together in a jar. (Crispy Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry)
- [ ] Trim the green beans. Refrigerate. (Crispy Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry)
- [ ] Halve the cherry tomatoes and finely dice the onion. Refrigerate. (Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls)
- [ ] Dice the onion. Refrigerate. (Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup)
