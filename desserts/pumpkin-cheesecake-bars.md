# Pumpkin Cheesecake Bars

*Gluten-free, with a gingersnap crust. Baked in the 9×13 glass Pyrex with no water bath. Makes 24 bars.*

## Ingredients
### Crust
- 2 cups gluten-free gingersnap crumbs (about 8–9 oz of cookies, such as Mi-Del, Kinnikinnick or Schär; regular gingersnaps contain wheat)
- 5 tbsp butter, melted
- pinch of salt

### Filling (everything at room temperature)
- 24 oz cream cheese (3 blocks), softened
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin purée (not pumpkin pie filling)
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice (or 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp nutmeg and a pinch of cloves)
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Directions
1. **Prep.** Heat the oven to 325°F. Line the dish with parchment so it hangs over the two long sides. You'll use the overhang to lift the bars out.
2. **Crust.** Crush the gingersnaps finely in a food processor, or in a zip-top bag with a rolling pin. Stir in the butter and salt, then press the mixture firmly into the bottom of the dish. Bake for 8–10 minutes; gingersnaps brown fast, so check at 8. Leave the oven on.
3. **Filling.** Beat the cream cheese on medium-low until smooth, about 2 minutes, scraping the bowl. Beat in both sugars and the cornstarch. Add the pumpkin, vanilla, spice and salt, and mix until smooth. Add the eggs one at a time **on low speed**, mixing only until each one disappears. Beating in extra air is what makes the top crack.
4. **Bake.** Pour the filling over the warm crust, smooth the top, and tap the dish on the counter a few times to pop any bubbles. Bake for 40–50 minutes. It's done when the edges are set and puffed slightly and the center 2–3 inches still jiggles a little when you nudge the dish.
5. **Cool slowly (this replaces the water bath).** Turn the oven off, prop the door open a few inches, and leave the bars inside for 30 minutes. Then cool them on the counter for about an hour.
6. **Chill** for at least 4 hours, or overnight (which is better). Lift the bars out with the parchment and cut them with a sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped clean between cuts.

## Notes
- **Glass dish:** 325°F is already the right temperature for glass, so don't lower it any further. Keep the hot dish off cold or wet surfaces, which can crack the glass.
- **Almond flour crust (if there are no gluten-free gingersnaps):** mix 2 cups almond flour, 1/4 cup brown sugar, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, a pinch of salt and 6 tbsp melted butter. Bake for 12–14 minutes until golden at the edges.
- **Gluten-free graham cracker crust:** mix 2 cups gluten-free graham crumbs, 1/4 cup sugar, a pinch of salt and 7 tbsp melted butter. Bake for 10–12 minutes.
- **7×11 dish:** use 2/3 of everything (2 blocks of cream cheese, 2 eggs, about 1 cup of pumpkin) and start checking for doneness at 35 minutes.
- **Toppings (optional):** whipped cream with cinnamon, or a drizzle of maple syrup.
- **Storage:** they keep 5 days in the fridge and freeze well. Wrap the bars one by one and thaw them in the fridge.
