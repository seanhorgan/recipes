# Planning Protocols

## Family Profile
- **Total:** 4 People (Ali, Sean, Rose, Will).
- **Kids:** Rose & Will (11 years old - Active Growth Phase).
- **Dietary (Sean):** Pescatarian-leaning. Fish is the preferred animal protein; poultry is fine occasionally. No red meat or pork.
- **Dietary (Ali):** Gluten-Free (GF). Default to everyone eating GF for the main shared dish.
- **Preferences (Will):** Dislikes mushrooms.
- **Everyone** enjoys plant proteins like tofu, beans, and lentils.

## Kid-Specific Nutrient Density (PRIORITY)
- **Growth Spurt Support:** Proactively boost protein and micronutrients (Iron, Calcium, Healthy Fats) in every meal.
- **Invisible Protein:** Look for ways to add protein to the shared meal that the adults might not otherwise prioritize:
    - Mash beans/chickpeas into sauces or ricotta bases.
    - Add hemp seeds, nutritional yeast, or ground flax to grain bowls and pastas.
    - Increase cheese portions (Parmesan/Ricotta) or add a side of Greek yogurt-based sauce.
- **Scaling:** If a meal seems "light," suggest a high-protein side or snack (e.g., hard-boiled eggs, edamame) for the kids specifically. Record it in the recipe's `## Kid Boost`.
- **Healthy defaults:** Avoid too much fat, sugar, and processed ingredients.

## Scheduling & Prep
- **Sunday Prep focus:** Optimize for meals that can be partially or fully prepped on Sunday.
- **Fast Weeknight Execution:** Aim for 10-15 minute active assembly/cook time.
- **Unified Meals:** Plan exactly ONE main meal for the family, with optional protein "boosters" for the kids.

## Variety
- **Meal Variety:** Each night in a weekly plan should be a different meal type (e.g., only one flatbread night per
  week, only one heavy shrimp night). Avoid repeating a dish format (a recipe's `tags`, like `flatbread` or `stir-fry`)
  within the same week.
- **Avoid repeats:** Don't repeat a recipe cooked in the last 4 weeks, and don't use the same `key` ingredient (see
  `ingredients.md`) twice in one week. *(Proposed default; adjust as needed.)*
- **Mix proteins:** Aim for a mix across the week, for example 2 fish/seafood, 2 plant, and at most 1 poultry.
  *(Proposed default; adjust as needed.)*
- **Last cooked:** Work out when a recipe was last cooked from the weekly plan files. Recipes don't store it.
- **Favor favorites:** Recipes rated ★★★★ or more are good candidates to bring back after a few weeks.

## Scaling
- **Family Sizing:** Default to 5 large sweet potatoes (1 per person + 1 extra).
- **Kid Preferences:** Substitute Tahini with [Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce](../sauces/lemon-garlic-yogurt-sauce.md).
- **Preparation:** Use unpeeled yellow potatoes for mashes.
