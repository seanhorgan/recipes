# Black Bean & Guacamole Rice Bowls

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked brown rice
- 2 cans black beans, rinsed
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 yellow onion, finely diced
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1 cup guacamole
- Optional: 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the brown rice for the bowls: bring 2/3 cup brown rice and 1 1/2 cups water to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 45 mins (makes 2 cups cooked). Refrigerate.
2. Halve 1 cup cherry tomatoes and finely dice 1/2 yellow onion. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the rice.
2. Simmer the black beans with the cumin and half the salsa for 5 mins until hot and slightly thickened.
3. Divide the rice among bowls. Top with the beans, tomatoes, onion, guacamole, and the remaining salsa.
