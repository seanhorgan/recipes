# 15-Minute Black Bean Quesadillas

*Pantry-staple quesadillas with almost no chopping.*

## Ingredients
- 2 cans black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 pack flour tortillas
- 1-2 cups shredded Mexican blend cheese, or cheddar
- olive oil, or cooking spray, for the pan

## Weeknight
1. In a medium bowl, mash the black beans slightly with a fork. Stir in the salsa and cumin.
2. Spoon the bean mixture onto one half of each tortilla. Top with cheese and fold the tortilla over.
3. Heat a large skillet over medium heat with a light drizzle of olive oil or cooking spray.
4. Cook the quesadillas 3–4 mins per side, until golden and crisp and the cheese is melted. Cut into wedges and serve.

## Notes
- Gluten-free: use GF flour tortillas.
