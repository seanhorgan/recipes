# Black Bean Quesadillas

*Pantry-staple quesadillas with almost no chopping.*

## Ingredients
- 2 cans black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup salsa, plus more for serving
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 pack flour tortillas, 8 tortillas (8-inch)
- 2 cups shredded Mexican blend cheese, or cheddar
- 1 tbsp olive oil, or cooking spray, for the pan
- 1 avocado, sliced, for serving
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, for serving

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 200°F to keep the finished quesadillas warm.
2. In a medium bowl, mash the black beans roughly with a fork or potato masher, leaving some whole. Stir in the salsa, cumin, and garlic powder.
3. Lay out the tortillas. Spread about 1/3 cup of the bean mixture over one half of each, top with 1/4 cup cheese, and fold the tortilla over.
4. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and brush lightly with olive oil. Cook 2 quesadillas at a time for 3 mins per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until golden and crisp and the cheese is melted.
5. Move each batch to a sheet pan in the warm oven and repeat with the rest, brushing the pan with a little oil each time.
6. Cut each quesadilla into 3 wedges. Serve with the avocado, Greek yogurt (as sour cream), and extra salsa.

## Kid Boost
- The Greek yogurt in place of sour cream adds protein; serve 2 quesadillas per kid if they're hungry.

## Notes
- Gluten-free: use GF flour tortillas.
