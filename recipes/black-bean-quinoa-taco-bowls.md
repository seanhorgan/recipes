# Black Bean & Quinoa Taco Bowls with Avocado

## Ingredients
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1.5 cups cooked quinoa
- 2 tbsp GF taco seasoning
- 1/2 cup vegetable broth, or water
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup canned sweet corn, rinsed and drained
- 1 large avocado, diced
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- fresh cilantro

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.
2. Halve the cherry tomatoes.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the quinoa.
2. In a medium skillet, simmer the black beans, taco seasoning, and broth over medium heat for 5 mins until heated through and slightly thickened.
3. Divide the quinoa among four bowls. Top with the beans, tomatoes, corn, and avocado.
4. Garnish with cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
