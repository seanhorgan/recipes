# Black Bean & Roasted Sweet Potato Power Bowls

## Ingredients
- 2 cans black beans, rinsed
- 3 large sweet potatoes, cubed
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- lime juice
- pumpkin seeds
- 1 batch [Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce](../sauces/lemon-garlic-yogurt-sauce.md)

## Sunday Prep
1. Toss the cubed sweet potatoes with olive oil and salt; roast at 400°F until tender. Refrigerate.
2. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Briefly warm the pre-roasted sweet potatoes and quinoa in a skillet.
2. Stir in the black beans and a splash of lime juice.
3. Serve topped with a generous dollop of Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce and pumpkin seeds.
