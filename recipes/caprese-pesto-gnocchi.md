# Caprese Pesto Gnocchi

*Shelf-stable gnocchi tossed with pesto, tomatoes, and mozzarella.*

## Ingredients
- 2 packages potato gnocchi, shelf-stable (about 16 oz each)
- 1/2 cup basil pesto
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 package fresh mozzarella pearls, drained

## Sunday Prep
1. Halve the cherry tomatoes. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the gnocchi per the package (usually 2–3 mins, until they float).
2. Drain well, reserving a couple of tablespoons of cooking water if needed.
3. Return the warm gnocchi to the pot. Toss right away with the pesto, tomatoes, and mozzarella until the cheese softens slightly.
4. Serve immediately.

## Kid Boost
- Light on protein: add a side of edamame, or toss in a can of rinsed cannellini beans.

## Notes
- Gluten-free: most shelf-stable gnocchi contains wheat; buy a GF potato gnocchi.
