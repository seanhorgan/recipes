# Chicken Sausage & White Bean Cassoulet

## Ingredients
- 12 oz GF chicken sausage, sliced
- 2 cans white beans, rinsed
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- leeks, sliced
- cooked quinoa

## Sunday Prep
1. Slice and sauté the leeks until soft. Refrigerate.
2. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Sauté the sausage with the prepped leeks.
2. Add the beans and tomatoes. Simmer for 10 mins.
3. Serve in deep bowls over warm quinoa.

## Notes
- Check the sausage label to make sure it's gluten-free.
