# Chickpea & Spinach Curry

*A cozy coconut curry served over rice.*

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp yellow curry powder
- 2 cans chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 1 can coconut milk, full-fat (13.5 oz)
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 5 oz baby spinach
- salt and pepper
- instant rice, prepared according to the package

## Sunday Prep
1. Dice the onion, mince the garlic, and grate the ginger. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or saucepan over medium heat. Sauté the onion 4–5 mins until soft.
2. Stir in the garlic, ginger, and curry powder. Cook about 1 min until fragrant.
3. Add the chickpeas, coconut milk, and broth. Simmer gently about 10 mins, until the sauce thickens slightly.
4. Stir in the spinach and cook 2 mins until wilted. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Serve over the rice.
