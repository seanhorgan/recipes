# Coconut Green Lentil & Spinach Soup

## Ingredients
- 3 cups cooked green lentils
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp cumin
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 2 cups cooked brown rice
- salt and pepper
- Optional: 2 cups water, or vegetable broth for a soupier dinner

## Sunday Prep
1. Dice the onion. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Sauté the onion in the olive oil for 4 mins. Stir in the turmeric, ginger, and cumin for 1 min.
2. Add the lentils, coconut milk, and water. Simmer for 8 mins.
3. Stir in the spinach and rice for 2 mins until the spinach wilts. Season with salt and pepper.

## Kid Boost
- Stir in 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds or a spoonful of Greek yogurt on top.
