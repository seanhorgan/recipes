# Creamy Butternut Squash & White Bean Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 lb butternut squash, cubed (two 20 oz packages of pre-cut cubes)
- 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, rinsed
- 4 cups vegetable broth, gluten-free
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, for serving
- 1 GF baguette, cut into 1/2-inch slices

## Sunday Prep
1. Roast the squash: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 2 lb cubed butternut squash with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 30–35 mins, flipping halfway, until tender and browned at the edges. Cool. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. In a large pot, combine the roasted squash, 1 can of the beans, 4 cups vegetable broth, and 1 tsp dried thyme. Bring to a boil over high heat, then lower to medium-low and simmer, partly covered, for 10 mins.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 400°F. Lay the baguette slices on a sheet pan, brush with 1 tbsp olive oil, and bake 6–8 mins until golden.
3. Take the soup off the heat and blend with an immersion blender until smooth.
4. Stir in the second can of beans, left whole, and simmer 3 mins to warm through. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Serve each bowl topped with 2 tbsp Parmesan, with the crostini on the side.

## Kid Boost
- Spread the crostini with ricotta, or serve a hard-boiled egg on the side.
