# Creamy White Bean & Spinach Soup

## Ingredients
- 3 cans (15 oz) cannellini beans, 2 rinsed and 1 unrinsed for extra creaminess
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 cup Parmesan, grated, for serving
- 1 loaf GF crusty bread, for serving
- 3/4 tsp salt, to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Sunday Prep
1. Dice 1 yellow onion and mince 3 cloves garlic. Refrigerate together in a small container.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 350°F for the bread.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion for 4–5 mins, stirring, until soft and translucent. Stir in the garlic and thyme for 1 min until fragrant.
3. Add the broth and all the beans (with the liquid from the unrinsed can). Bring to a gentle boil over medium-high, then lower the heat and simmer for 8 mins.
4. While the soup simmers, slice the bread, wrap it in foil, and warm it in the oven for 8–10 mins.
5. Turn off the heat. Blend about half the soup with an immersion blender (or blend 2 cups in a blender, carefully, and stir it back in), leaving the rest chunky.
6. Stir in the spinach for 2 mins until wilted. Stir in the lemon juice, salt, and pepper; taste and add more salt if needed.
7. Ladle into bowls, top each with 2 tbsp Parmesan, and serve with the warm bread.

## Kid Boost
- The Parmesan topping adds protein and calcium; a hard-boiled egg on the side rounds it out.

## Notes
- The whole soup can be made on Sunday; stir in the spinach when reheating.
