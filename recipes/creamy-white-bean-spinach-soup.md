# Creamy White Bean & Spinach Soup

## Ingredients
- 3 cans (15 oz) cannellini beans, 2 rinsed and 1 unrinsed for extra creaminess
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 loaf GF crusty bread, for serving
- salt and pepper

## Sunday Prep
1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion for 4 mins until translucent. Stir in the garlic and thyme for 1 min.
2. Add the broth and all the beans. Bring to a gentle boil, then simmer for 8 mins.
3. Blend about half the soup with an immersion blender, leaving the rest chunky.
4. Stir in the spinach for 2 mins until wilted. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Serve with warm slices of GF bread.

## Notes
- The whole soup can be made on Sunday; stir in the spinach when reheating.
