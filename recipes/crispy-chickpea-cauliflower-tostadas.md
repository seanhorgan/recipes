# Crispy Chickpea & Cauliflower Tostadas

*High-fiber with a crunch; boosted for growth spurt support.*

## Ingredients
- 2 lb cauliflower florets
- 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, rinsed
- 3 tbsp olive oil, plus 1 tsp for the skillet
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 8 corn tostada shells, gluten-free
- 2 avocados
- 1 lime
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, or crumbled feta, for protein

## Sunday Prep
1. Roast the cauliflower and chickpeas: heat the oven to 400°F. Cut any large cauliflower florets into bite-size pieces and pat 2 cans rinsed chickpeas dry. Toss both on 2 parchment-lined sheet pans with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 3/4 tsp salt. Roast 25 mins, stirring halfway, until browned at the edges. Cool. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat 1 tsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the cauliflower and chickpeas and cook 5 mins, stirring now and then, until hot and crisp again.
2. Mash 2 avocados in a bowl with the juice of 1/2 lime and 1/4 tsp salt. Cut the other lime half into wedges.
3. Spread about 2 tbsp avocado on each of the 8 tostada shells. Top each with about 3/4 cup of the cauliflower and chickpeas, 1 tbsp salsa, and 1 tbsp Greek yogurt (or feta).
4. Serve 2 tostadas each, with the lime wedges.
