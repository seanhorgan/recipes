# Crispy Halloumi with Roasted Broccoli & Potatoes

*Golden, squeaky halloumi roasted with broccoli, potatoes, and tomatoes, with the kids' lemon-garlic yogurt sauce.*

## Ingredients
- 2 blocks halloumi, 8 oz each, cut into 1/2-inch slices
- 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 2 bags broccoli florets, 10 oz each
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- salt and pepper
- 1 batch [Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce](../sauces/lemon-garlic-yogurt-sauce.md)

## Sunday Prep
1. Par-roast the potatoes: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 1.5 lb halved baby potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 20 mins until just tender. Cool. Refrigerate.
2. Cut 1 red onion into wedges. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Spread the potatoes, broccoli, onion, and cherry tomatoes over 2 parchment-lined sheet pans. Toss with 2 tbsp olive oil, the smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Roast 12 mins.
2. Nestle the halloumi slices among the vegetables and roast 10–12 mins more, until the halloumi is golden at the edges and the broccoli is charred in spots. (For extra color, broil the last 2 mins.)
3. Serve with plenty of Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce.

## Kid Boost
- Double the yogurt sauce: it adds protein and calcium.

## Notes
- Halloumi is salty, so don't salt it; season only the vegetables.
- About 24 g protein per serving from the halloumi alone.
