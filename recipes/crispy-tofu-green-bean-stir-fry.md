# Crispy Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry

## Ingredients
- 1-2 blocks (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp avocado oil, divided
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 2 cups cooked brown rice
### Sauce
- 3 tbsp tamari
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

## Sunday Prep
1. Press the tofu and cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Refrigerate.
2. Whisk the tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil together in a jar.
3. Trim the green beans. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Toss the tofu in cornstarch. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the tofu for 6-8 mins, turning, until golden and crispy. Remove from the pan.
2. Add the remaining oil and the green beans with a splash of water. Cover and cook 2 mins, then uncover and stir-fry 3 mins until tender-crisp.
3. Return the tofu to the pan with the rice and pour the sauce over everything. Toss for 2 mins until hot and coated.
