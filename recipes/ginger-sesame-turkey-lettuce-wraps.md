# Ginger-Sesame Turkey Lettuce Wraps

*Savory ground turkey with carrots, crunchy cashews, and a ginger-tamari glaze, spooned into butter lettuce cups.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 2 carrots, shredded
- 1 bell pepper, finely diced
- 2 heads butter lettuce, leaves separated
- 1/2 cup cashews, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
### Sauce
- 3 tbsp tamari
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

## Sunday Prep
1. Shred the carrots and finely dice the bell pepper. Refrigerate together.
2. Whisk the tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil in a jar. Refrigerate.
3. Wash and separate the lettuce leaves, pat dry, and refrigerate wrapped in a paper towel.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the avocado oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the turkey and break it into small crumbles. Cook 6–7 mins until no pink remains.
2. Add the carrots and bell pepper and cook 2–3 mins until just tender.
3. Pour in the sauce and toss for 1 min until glossy. Stir in the cashews.
4. Spoon into the lettuce cups and serve with lime wedges.

## Kid Boost
- Side of shelled edamame, or serve the turkey over a scoop of brown rice for extra fuel.
