# Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers

## Ingredients
- 4 bell peppers, halved lengthwise and seeded
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 cup cooked quinoa (from 1/3 cup dry)
- 1 cup tomato sauce (one 8 oz can)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa: rinse 1/3 cup quinoa, bring to a boil with 2/3 cup water, cover, and simmer on low 15 mins. Rest 5 mins covered, then fluff (makes about 1 cup).
2. Make the filling: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 lb ground turkey, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper. Break into small crumbles and cook 6–7 mins until no pink remains. Off the heat, stir in the quinoa and 1 cup tomato sauce.
3. Stuff the peppers: halve 4 bell peppers lengthwise through the stem and pull out the seeds and ribs. Set them cut-side up in a 9x13-inch baking dish and fill each half with about 1/2 cup filling. Cover with foil and refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Bake the peppers covered with foil for 20 mins (25 mins straight from the fridge).
3. Uncover and bake 10 mins more, until the peppers are tender and the tops are browned. Serve 2 halves each.

## Kid Boost
- Sprinkle 1/4 cup grated Parmesan over the peppers when you uncover them, or serve with a spoonful of Greek yogurt.
