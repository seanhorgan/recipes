# Greek-Style Stuffed Bell Peppers

## Ingredients
- 4 bell peppers, halved
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa.
2. Brown the turkey with the oregano. Mix with the quinoa and tomato sauce.
3. Stuff the peppers, cover, and refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Bake the stuffed peppers at 375°F for 25 mins (a few minutes longer straight from the fridge).
