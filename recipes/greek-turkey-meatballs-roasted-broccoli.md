# Greek Turkey Meatballs with Roasted Broccoli

*Tender turkey-spinach meatballs (quinoa instead of breadcrumbs keeps them gluten-free) roasted on one pan with broccoli, served over quinoa with the kids' lemon-garlic yogurt sauce.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 2 tbsp Greek yogurt (instead of an egg, to bind)
- 2 cups cooked quinoa, divided (1/2 cup for the meatballs, 1 1/2 cups for serving)
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 cup spinach, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 bag (10 oz) broccoli florets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 batch [Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce](../sauces/lemon-garlic-yogurt-sauce.md)

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa: rinse 2/3 cup quinoa, bring to a boil with 1 1/3 cups water, cover, and simmer on low 15 mins. Rest 5 mins covered, then fluff (makes about 2 cups). Cool. Refrigerate.
2. Make the meatballs: in a large bowl, mix 1 lb ground turkey, 2 tbsp Greek yogurt, 1/2 cup cooled cooked quinoa, 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, 1 cup finely chopped spinach, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper with your hands until just combined. Roll into 16 balls (about 2 tbsp each). Set them on one half of a parchment-lined sheet pan, cover, and refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the broccoli florets with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/4 tsp salt on the empty half of the sheet pan with the meatballs.
3. Roast 16–18 mins, until the meatballs are browned and cooked through (165°F inside) and the broccoli edges are crisp.
4. While it roasts, warm the remaining 1 1/2 cups quinoa in the microwave with 1 tbsp water, covered, for 2 mins.
5. Serve the meatballs and broccoli over the quinoa with the yogurt sauce spooned on top.

## Notes
- Serves 3–4. For 4 hungry eaters, add a side of cucumber slices or a second bag of broccoli.
