# Hearty Shakshuka with Feta

## Ingredients
- 1 jar (23 oz) marinara sauce, or crushed tomatoes
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 6-8 eggs
- 1 block feta, crumbled
- cumin
- GF bread, for serving

## Sunday Prep
1. Dice the bell peppers. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Simmer the marinara sauce with the diced bell peppers and a pinch of cumin.
2. Crack the eggs into the sauce; cover with a tight lid and cook on medium-low until the whites are set.
3. Top with crumbled feta and serve with toasted GF bread.
