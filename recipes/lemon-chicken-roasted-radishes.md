# Lemon Chicken & Roasted Radishes

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts
- 1 bunch radishes, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- fresh thyme
- salt and pepper

## Sunday Prep
1. Halve the radishes. Refrigerate.
2. Whisk the lemon juice and mustard together for the pan drizzle.

## Weeknight
1. Toss the radishes in olive oil and spread on a sheet pan alongside the chicken, seasoned with thyme, salt, and pepper.
2. Roast at 400°F for 20–25 mins until the chicken is cooked through and the radishes are tender.
3. Drizzle with the lemon-mustard sauce.
