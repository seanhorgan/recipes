# Lemon-Garlic White Fish & Kale Skillet

*A quick, high-iron pescatarian skillet with bright citrus notes.*

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb white fish (cod, tilapia, or halibut), cut into bite-sized chunks
- 1 bunch kale, chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 batch [Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce](../sauces/lemon-garlic-yogurt-sauce.md)
- 1 can cannellini beans, rinsed (added for kid growth/protein)

## Sunday Prep
1. Wash and chop the kale.

## Weeknight
1. Sauté the fish chunks in olive oil until golden and just cooked through (about 5–6 mins). Remove the fish from the pan.
2. Add the kale, beans, and 2 tbsp water to the pan; cover for 2 mins to steam the kale.
3. Return the fish to the pan and pour in the yogurt sauce.
4. Simmer for 1 min until everything is coated and warm.

## Notes
- Zing Dressing (Jar A) works instead of the yogurt sauce.
- Chicken option: sear 4 thin-cut chicken breasts in olive oil for 5–6 mins per side until cooked through, then wilt the kale with 3 cloves minced garlic and finish with the juice of 2 lemons.
