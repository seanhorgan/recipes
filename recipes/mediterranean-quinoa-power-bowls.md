# Mediterranean Quinoa Power Bowls

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup Kalamata olives
- 2 oz feta
- olive oil
- lemon juice
- honey

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.
2. Dice the cucumber and halve the tomatoes.
3. Shake olive oil, lemon juice, and a little honey together in a jar for the dressing.

## Weeknight
1. Toss the quinoa and chickpeas with the vegetables and olives.
2. Top with feta and the dressing.
