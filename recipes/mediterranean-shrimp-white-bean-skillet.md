# Mediterranean Shrimp & White Bean Skillet Bake

*A one-pan braise using heart-healthy fats and quick protein.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 cans white beans, rinsed
- 1 jar roasted red peppers, sliced
- 1 bag spinach or kale
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup feta
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- olive oil

## Weeknight
1. Sauté the garlic in a large skillet with olive oil.
2. Add the white beans and roasted red peppers. Simmer for 5 mins, lightly smashing some beans to thicken the base.
3. Stir in the greens until wilted.
4. Nestle the shrimp into the mixture. Cover and cook for 4–5 mins until the shrimp are pink.
5. Top with crumbled feta and a squeeze of lemon.
