# Peanut Rice Noodles with Edamame & Scrambled Egg

*Warm rice noodles tossed in a lime-peanut sauce with edamame, soft scrambled eggs, and crunchy cabbage and cucumber.*

## Ingredients
- 1 package GF rice noodles, 7.7 oz
- 2 cups edamame, frozen shelled
- 6 eggs
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 bag cabbage, shredded red
- 2 cucumbers, cut into matchsticks
- fresh cilantro
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Optional: 1/4 cup cashews, chopped
### Peanut sauce
- 1/3 cup peanut butter
- 3 tbsp tamari
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1/4 cup water

## Sunday Prep
1. Whisk the peanut sauce ingredients together until smooth. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Cook the rice noodles according to the package, adding the edamame for the last 3 mins. Drain and rinse briefly with warm water.
2. Meanwhile, heat the sesame oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt and scramble into soft curds, about 3 mins. Set aside.
3. Loosen the peanut sauce with a splash of hot water. Toss the noodles and edamame with the sauce, then fold in the eggs and half the cabbage.
4. Serve topped with the rest of the cabbage, the cucumber, cilantro, cashews, and lime wedges.

## Notes
- About 25 g protein per serving from the eggs, edamame, and peanut sauce.
- Leftovers are good cold for lunch.
