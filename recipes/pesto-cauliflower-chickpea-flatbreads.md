# Pesto-Roasted Cauliflower & Chickpea Flatbreads

*A twist on the Zucchini Flatbreads using the twins' favorite roasted veggies and pesto.*

## Ingredients
- 2-4 GF flatbreads
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into small florets
- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
- 1/2 cup GF pesto
- 1/2 cup ricotta or goat cheese
- 1 handful arugula
- olive oil

## Sunday Prep
1. Toss the cauliflower and chickpeas with olive oil and salt. Roast at 400°F for 20 mins until crispy. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Spread a thin layer of ricotta or goat cheese on the flatbreads.
2. Top with the roasted cauliflower and chickpeas.
3. Drizzle the pesto generously over the top.
4. Bake at 425°F for 5–8 mins until the flatbread is crisp.
5. Finish with fresh arugula.
