# Pesto-Roasted Cauliflower & Chickpea Grain Bowls

*A high-fiber, assembly-style bowl using the twins' favorite roasted veggies and pesto.*

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked quinoa or sorghum
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into small florets
- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
- 1/2 cup GF pesto
- 1 handful arugula
- olive oil
- Optional: crumbled goat cheese or feta

## Sunday Prep
1. Toss the cauliflower and chickpeas with olive oil and salt. Roast at 400°F for 20 mins until crispy. Refrigerate.
2. Cook the quinoa or sorghum. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the pre-cooked grains.
2. In a large bowl, layer the grains and top with a big handful of arugula.
3. Add the roasted cauliflower and chickpeas.
4. Drizzle the pesto generously over the top and toss lightly.
5. Optional: top with a sprinkle of cheese.
