# Red Lentil Soup & Polenta Fries

## Ingredients
- 1 quart red lentil soup
- 1 bag spinach
- 1 tube polenta
- olive oil
- 1 apple, sliced
- 1/4 cup walnuts
- 2 oz feta

## Sunday Prep
1. Slice the polenta into fries; store covered in the fridge.

## Weeknight
1. Toss the polenta fries with oil and bake at 400°F for 20 mins.
2. Reheat the soup and stir in the spinach to wilt.
3. Serve with an apple, walnut, and feta salad.
