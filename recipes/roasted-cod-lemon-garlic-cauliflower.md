# Roasted Cod & Lemon-Garlic Cauliflower

*Cod baked right on a tray of crisp lemon-garlic cauliflower, served over quinoa.*

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb cod fillets, cut into 4 portions
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into 1-inch florets (about 6 cups)
- 2 lemons, 1 juiced and 1 cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp garlic powder, divided
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 1/4 tsp salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup quinoa, uncooked (makes about 3 cups cooked)
- 2 cups water, for the quinoa
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/4 cup Parmesan, grated

## Sunday Prep
1. Rinse the quinoa in a fine-mesh strainer. Bring it to a boil with the 2 cups water and 1/4 tsp salt, cover, and simmer on low for 15 mins. Turn off the heat, rest 5 mins covered, fluff with a fork, cool, and refrigerate.
2. Cut the cauliflower into 1-inch florets, skipping this if you bought pre-cut florets. Store in a zip-top bag in the fridge.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
2. On the pan, toss the cauliflower with 2 tbsp olive oil, the lemon juice, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper. Spread in one layer and roast for 15 mins.
3. Meanwhile, pat the cod dry with paper towels. Rub with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and season with the remaining 1/2 tsp garlic powder, the smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper.
4. Stir the cauliflower, push it to the edges, and lay the cod in the middle of the pan. Roast 10–12 mins more, until the fish flakes easily with a fork (145°F inside).
5. While the cod roasts, warm the quinoa in the microwave, covered, with 1 tbsp water for 2 mins, stirring once.
6. Sprinkle the cauliflower with the Parmesan for the last 2 mins in the oven.
7. Serve the cod and cauliflower over the quinoa, topped with parsley and the lemon wedges for squeezing.

## Kid Boost
- The Parmesan on the cauliflower adds protein and calcium; add a side of shelled edamame for extra.
