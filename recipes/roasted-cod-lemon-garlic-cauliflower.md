# Roasted Cod & Lemon-Garlic Cauliflower

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb cod fillets
- 1 head cauliflower, chopped
- 1 lemon
- olive oil
- garlic powder
- 2 cups cooked quinoa

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Toss the cauliflower with lemon juice, oil, and garlic powder; roast at 400°F for 20 mins.
2. Place the cod fillets directly on the cauliflower tray for the final 10–12 mins.
3. Serve over a bed of warm quinoa.
