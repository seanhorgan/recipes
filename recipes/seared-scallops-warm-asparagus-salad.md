# Seared Scallops with Warm Asparagus Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 lb sea scallops
- 1 bunch asparagus, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- 1 lemon, zested
- 2 tbsp butter or olive oil

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.
2. Cut the asparagus into 1-inch pieces.

## Weeknight
1. Pat the scallops dry. Sear in a hot skillet with the butter or oil for 2 mins per side until a golden crust forms.
2. In the same pan, sauté the asparagus for 3 mins.
3. Serve the scallops and asparagus over warm quinoa with the lemon zest.
