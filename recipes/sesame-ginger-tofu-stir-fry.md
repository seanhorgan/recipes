# Crispy Sesame-Ginger Tofu Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles

## Ingredients
- 2 blocks (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp avocado oil, divided
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 cups sugar snap peas
- 8 oz GF rice noodles, pad thai or thin style
### Sauce
- 1/4 cup tamari
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

## Sunday Prep
1. Press the tofu and cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Refrigerate.
2. Whisk the tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil together in a jar.
3. Cut the broccoli and slice the bell pepper. Refrigerate together with the snap peas.

## Weeknight
1. Cook the rice noodles according to package instructions, drain, and set aside.
2. Toss the tofu in cornstarch. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Cook the tofu for 6–8 mins, turning occasionally, until golden and crispy on all sides. Remove from the pan.
3. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the broccoli, bell pepper, and snap peas for 4–5 mins until tender-crisp.
4. Return the tofu and noodles to the pan, pour the sauce over everything, and toss gently for 2 mins until hot and coated. Serve immediately.
