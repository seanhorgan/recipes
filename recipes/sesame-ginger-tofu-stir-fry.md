# Crispy Sesame-Ginger Tofu Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles

## Ingredients
- 2 blocks (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp avocado oil, divided
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 cups sugar snap peas
- 8 oz GF rice noodles, pad thai or thin style
### Sauce
- 1/4 cup tamari
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

## Sunday Prep
1. Drain 2 blocks (14 oz each) extra-firm tofu, wrap each block in a clean kitchen towel, and press under a heavy pan for 15 mins. Cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Refrigerate in a container lined with a paper towel.
2. Make the sauce: grate 1 tbsp fresh ginger and mince 2 cloves garlic. Whisk them in a jar with 1/4 cup tamari, 2 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, and 1 tsp toasted sesame oil. Refrigerate.
3. Cut 3 cups broccoli into bite-size florets and slice 1 red bell pepper into thin strips. Refrigerate together in a zip-top bag with 2 cups sugar snap peas.

## Weeknight
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the rice noodles according to the package (usually 4–6 mins), drain, rinse under cold water so they don't stick, and set aside.
2. Meanwhile, pat the tofu dry and toss it in a bowl with the cornstarch and salt until evenly coated.
3. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large nonstick skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the tofu in one layer and cook 6–8 mins, turning every 2 mins, until golden and crispy on all sides. Move it to a plate.
4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the broccoli, bell pepper, and snap peas for 4–5 mins until bright and tender-crisp. Add 2 tbsp water if the pan gets dry.
5. Shake the sauce. Return the tofu and noodles to the pan, pour the sauce over everything, and toss gently with tongs for 2 mins until hot and coated. Serve immediately.
