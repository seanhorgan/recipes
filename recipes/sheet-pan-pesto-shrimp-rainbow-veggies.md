# Sheet-Pan Pesto Shrimp & Rainbow Veggies

## Ingredients
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 4 tbsp GF pesto
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- salt and pepper

## Sunday Prep
1. Slice the peppers, cut the onion into wedges, and trim the asparagus. Refrigerate together.

## Weeknight
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Toss the peppers, onion, and asparagus with olive oil and salt on a large sheet pan. Roast for 10 mins.
2. Move the veggies to the side, add the shrimp and pesto, and toss to coat.
3. Roast 5–7 more mins until the shrimp are pink.
