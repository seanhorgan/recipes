# Sheet Pan Salmon & Asparagus

*A simple lemony sheet-pan dinner where the oven does the work.*

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1-2 lemons, juiced, plus wedges for serving
- salt and pepper

## Sunday Prep
1. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Arrange the salmon and asparagus on a large baking sheet.
3. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice over both. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Roast 12–15 mins, until the salmon flakes easily and the asparagus is tender-crisp. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes
- A lighter, potato-free cousin of [Lemon-Dill Salmon & Asparagus](lemon-dill-salmon-asparagus.md).
