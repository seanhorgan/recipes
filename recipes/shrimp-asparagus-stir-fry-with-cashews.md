# Shrimp & Asparagus Stir-Fry with Cashews

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined, thawed if frozen
- 1 bunch asparagus
- 3/4 cup cashews
- 1 batch [Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze](../sauces/honey-mustard-citrus-glaze.md), about 1/2 cup
- 1 tbsp avocado oil, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 cups instant rice, uncooked

## Sunday Prep
1. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus and cut the spears into 1 1/2-inch pieces. Refrigerate in a zip-top bag.

## Weeknight
1. Cook the instant rice per the package (usually 2 cups rice with 2 cups water, covered, 5 mins off the heat). Fluff and keep covered.
2. Pat the shrimp very dry with paper towels and season with the salt and pepper.
3. Toast the cashews in a large dry skillet or wok over medium heat for 2–3 mins, shaking the pan, until golden. Tip them into a bowl.
4. Raise the heat to medium-high and add 1/2 tbsp avocado oil. Add the shrimp in one layer and cook 1 1/2–2 mins per side until pink and just opaque. Move them to a plate.
5. Add the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil and the asparagus. Stir-fry 3–4 mins until bright green and tender-crisp, adding the garlic for the last 30 seconds.
6. Shake the glaze. Return the shrimp to the pan with the cashews and 1/3 cup of the glaze. Toss for 1 min until everything is hot and glossy.
7. Serve over the rice, with the rest of the glaze at the table for drizzling.
