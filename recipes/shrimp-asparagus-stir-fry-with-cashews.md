# Shrimp & Asparagus Stir-Fry with Cashews

## Ingredients
- 1 lb shrimp
- 1 bunch asparagus
- 3/4 cup cashews
- 2-3 tbsp [Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze](../sauces/honey-mustard-citrus-glaze.md)

## Sunday Prep
1. Trim and chop the asparagus.

## Weeknight
1. Sauté the shrimp 2 mins per side; remove.
2. Sauté the asparagus for 4 mins.
3. Return the shrimp to the pan with the cashews and glaze. Toss for 1 min until everything is coated and sticky.
