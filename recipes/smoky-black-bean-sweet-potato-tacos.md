# Smoky Black Bean & Sweet Potato Tacos

## Ingredients
- 4 sweet potatoes, cubed
- olive oil
- 1 can black beans, rinsed
- 1 tsp cumin
- 8 corn tortillas
- 2 avocados
- pickled red onions

## Sunday Prep
1. Toss the cubed sweet potatoes with olive oil and salt; roast at 400°F for 20 mins until tender. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the sweet potatoes and beans with the cumin.
2. Serve in charred tortillas with mashed avocado and pickled onions.
