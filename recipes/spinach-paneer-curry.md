# Creamy Spinach & Paneer Curry

*A mild, palak paneer–style spinach sauce made on Sunday, finished with Greek yogurt and oven-golden paneer.*

## Ingredients
- 2 blocks paneer, about 7 oz each, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 10 oz spinach
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 cup crushed tomatoes
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- salt
- 1.5 cups brown rice
- Optional: GF flatbreads, warmed

## Sunday Prep
1. Make the spinach sauce: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat and cook the onion 6 mins until soft. Stir in the garlic, ginger, garam masala, cumin, and turmeric for 1 min. Add the crushed tomatoes and 1/2 tsp salt; simmer 5 mins. Add the spinach by handfuls until wilted. Blend smooth with 1/2 cup water. Cool. Refrigerate.
2. Cook the brown rice. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the paneer cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Bake 12–15 mins, turning once, until golden in spots.
2. Meanwhile, warm the spinach sauce in a large skillet until gently bubbling, and reheat the rice.
3. Take the sauce off the heat and stir in the Greek yogurt (off the heat so it stays creamy), then fold in the paneer.
4. Serve over the rice, with warm GF flatbreads if you like.

## Notes
- About 28 g protein per serving from the paneer and yogurt.
- Check that the garam masala has no added wheat (most single-brand blends are fine).
