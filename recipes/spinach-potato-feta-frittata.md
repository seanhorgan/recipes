# Spinach, Potato & Feta Frittata

*A thick, protein-packed frittata with roasted potatoes, finished in the oven, with a lemony arugula salad.*

## Ingredients
- 10 eggs
- 1/3 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 lb yellow potatoes, unpeeled, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced
- 5 oz spinach
- 4 oz feta, crumbled
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 3/4 tsp salt, divided
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups arugula
- 1/2 lemon

## Sunday Prep
1. Roast the potatoes: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss 1 lb cubed yellow potatoes and 1/2 diced yellow onion with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast 25 mins, stirring halfway, until tender and golden. Cool. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Whisk 10 eggs, 1/3 cup Greek yogurt, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper in a large bowl until smooth.
3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a 10- or 12-inch oven-safe nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the potatoes and onion and warm 2 mins. Add the spinach a handful at a time, stirring 1–2 mins until wilted.
4. Pour in the eggs and scatter 4 oz crumbled feta over the top. Cook without stirring for 2 mins, until the edges start to set.
5. Move the skillet to the oven and bake 12–15 mins, until the center is set and doesn't jiggle. Rest 3 mins, then cut into 8 wedges.
6. While it bakes, toss 4 cups arugula with 1 tbsp olive oil, the juice of 1/2 lemon, and a pinch of salt.
7. Serve 2 wedges each with the salad.
