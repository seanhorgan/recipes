# Spring Pea Quinoa "Risotto"

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked quinoa
- 1.5 cups frozen peas
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- leeks, sliced
- vegetable broth
- 1 lemon, zested

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.
2. Sauté the sliced leeks until soft. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the quinoa with a splash of broth.
2. Stir in the peas and pre-sautéed leeks.
3. Finish with the yogurt and lemon zest for a creamy texture.

## Kid Boost
- Stir in 1/4 cup grated Parmesan at the end.
