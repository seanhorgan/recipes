# Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Mediterranean Chickpeas

## Ingredients
- 4-6 large sweet potatoes
- 2 cans chickpeas, rinsed
- 1/2 cup feta
- arugula
- 1 batch [Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze](../sauces/honey-mustard-citrus-glaze.md)

## Sunday Prep
1. Roast the whole sweet potatoes at 400°F for 45 mins until tender. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Reheat the potatoes and slice into thick rounds or split open.
2. Toss the chickpeas with 2 tbsp of the glaze.
3. Serve "platter style": a bed of arugula topped with the sweet potato rounds and chickpeas, sprinkled with feta.
4. Drizzle generously with more glaze; keeping it on the platter keeps the textures distinct.
