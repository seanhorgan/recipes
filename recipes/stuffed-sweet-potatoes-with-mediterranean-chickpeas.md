# Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Mediterranean Chickpeas

## Ingredients
- 5 large sweet potatoes
- 2 cans chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup feta, crumbled
- 5 oz arugula
- 1 batch [Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze](../sauces/honey-mustard-citrus-glaze.md), about 1/2 cup
- Optional: 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds

## Sunday Prep
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Scrub 5 large sweet potatoes, prick each 4–5 times with a fork, and set them on a foil-lined sheet pan. Roast for 45–50 mins until a knife slides in easily. Cool, then refrigerate whole.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Cut the sweet potatoes in half lengthwise, set them cut side up on a lined sheet pan, and reheat for 12–15 mins until hot through. (Or microwave 2 halves at a time for 2–3 mins.)
2. Meanwhile, pat the chickpeas dry. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and add the chickpeas, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and salt. Cook 5–6 mins, stirring often, until warm and lightly crisp. Turn off the heat and toss with 2 tbsp of the glaze.
3. Toss the arugula with 1 tbsp of the glaze and spread it over a large platter.
4. Lay the sweet potato halves on the arugula, mash the flesh lightly with a fork, and spoon the chickpeas over and around them.
5. Scatter with the feta and pumpkin seeds, and drizzle the rest of the glaze over the top. Serve platter style so the textures stay distinct.

## Kid Boost
- A dollop of plain Greek yogurt on each potato adds protein and calcium.
