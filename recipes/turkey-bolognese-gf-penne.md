# Turkey Bolognese with GF Penne

*A hearty turkey and vegetable meat sauce made on Sunday, so the night is just boiling pasta.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey, 93% lean
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 carrots, peeled and finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jar (23.5 oz) marinara sauce
- 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes, optional
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 12 oz GF pasta, penne
- 3/4 cup Parmesan, grated, divided
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Sunday Prep
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and carrots with 1/2 tsp salt and cook 7–8 mins, stirring, until soft. Add the garlic for 1 min.
2. Raise the heat to medium-high, add the turkey, and break it into small crumbles with a wooden spoon. Cook 6–7 mins until no pink remains.
3. Stir in the marinara, crushed tomatoes, oregano, red pepper flakes, the remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and the pepper. Simmer uncovered on low for 15 mins, stirring now and then, until thick. Cool and refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil with 1 tbsp salt.
2. Meanwhile, reheat the sauce in a covered pot over medium-low for 10 mins, stirring occasionally, until bubbling. Add a splash of water if it's too thick.
3. Cook the pasta for 1 min less than the package time (usually 9–10 mins), stirring after the first minute so it doesn't stick. Scoop out 1/2 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
4. Add the pasta to the sauce with 1/2 cup Parmesan and a few spoonfuls of pasta water. Toss for 1 min until glossy.
5. Serve topped with the remaining Parmesan and the parsley.

## Kid Boost
- The extra Parmesan adds protein and calcium; a side of shelled edamame rounds it out.

## Notes
- GF pasta turns mushy if overcooked or left sitting, so cook it just before serving.
- The sauce freezes well; double it on Sunday for a future week.
