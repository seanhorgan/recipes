# Turkey & Sweet Potato Shepherd's Pie

*Savory turkey, carrots, and peas in a thyme gravy (thickened with cornstarch, so it's gluten-free) under a mashed sweet potato top. Assemble on Sunday and bake on the night.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 2 lb sweet potatoes (about 3 medium), peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 tbsp tamari
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan

## Sunday Prep
1. Make the sweet potato mash: put 2 lb peeled sweet potato chunks in a pot, cover with cold water, and add 1/2 tsp salt. Bring to a boil and simmer 12–15 mins until very tender. Drain well and mash with 2 tbsp butter and 1/4 tsp salt.
2. While the potatoes cook, dice 1 yellow onion and 2 carrots, and mince 2 cloves garlic.
3. Make the filling: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onion and carrots 5 mins until starting to soften. Add 1 lb ground turkey, 1 tsp dried thyme, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper. Break into small crumbles and cook 6–7 mins until no pink remains. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Whisk 1 cup vegetable broth, 1 tbsp tamari, and 1 tbsp cornstarch in a measuring cup. Pour into the skillet and simmer 2–3 mins, stirring, until thick and glossy. Stir in 1 cup frozen peas.
5. Assemble: spread the filling in a 9-inch square or 2-quart baking dish. Spread the sweet potato mash evenly over the top and sprinkle with 1/4 cup grated Parmesan. Cool, cover, and refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Uncover the dish and bake 25–30 mins, until bubbling at the edges and hot in the center. For a browner top, broil 2 mins at the end.
3. Rest 5 mins, then cut into 4 servings.
