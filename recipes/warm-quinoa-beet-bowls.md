# Warm Quinoa & Beet Bowls

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups quinoa
- 2 cans beets, rinsed and sliced
- 4 oz goat cheese
- 1/2 cup walnuts
- 2 cups arugula
- Zing Dressing (Jar A)

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the quinoa. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the quinoa and beets in a pan.
2. Place over a bed of arugula.
3. Top with crumbled goat cheese, walnuts, and a heavy drizzle of Jar A.
