# Warm Sorghum & Roasted Carrot Bowl

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked sorghum
- 1 lb carrots
- honey
- olive oil
- 2 oz feta
- fresh parsley

## Sunday Prep
1. Cook the sorghum. Refrigerate.
2. Slice the carrots into coins, toss with olive oil and honey, and roast at 400°F for 20 mins. Refrigerate.

## Weeknight
1. Warm the sorghum and carrots together.
2. Top with feta and chopped parsley.

## Kid Boost
- A light meal: add hard-boiled eggs or edamame on the side.
