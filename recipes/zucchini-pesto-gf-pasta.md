# Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta

## Ingredients
- 1 box (8–12 oz) GF pasta, ideally red lentil or chickpea fusilli for extra protein
- 2 zucchini (about 1 lb), spiralized
- 1/2 cup GF pesto
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
- 2 tbsp pine nuts
- 1 tbsp salt, for the pasta water

## Sunday Prep
1. Spiralize 2 zucchini (or cut them into thin ribbons with a vegetable peeler). Refrigerate in a container lined with a paper towel.
2. Toast 2 tbsp pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3–4 mins, shaking often, until golden. Cool and store at room temperature.

## Weeknight
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil with 1 tbsp salt. Cook the pasta per the package (usually 7–9 mins for red lentil pasta).
2. Add the zucchini to the pot for the last 2 mins.
3. Scoop out 1/2 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
4. Return the pasta and zucchini to the pot. Toss with 1/2 cup pesto, 1/4 cup Parmesan, and 2–4 tbsp of the pasta water until glossy.
5. Top with the toasted pine nuts.

## Kid Boost
- Red lentil or chickpea pasta roughly doubles the protein. Add a side of shelled edamame for more.
