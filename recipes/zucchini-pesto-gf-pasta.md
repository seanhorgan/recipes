# Zucchini & Pesto GF Pasta

## Ingredients
- 1 box GF fusilli
- 2 zucchini, spiralized
- 1/2 cup GF pesto
- 2 tbsp pine nuts

## Sunday Prep
1. Spiralize the zucchini. Toast the pine nuts.

## Weeknight
1. Boil the pasta. In the last 2 mins, add the zucchini to the water.
2. Drain and toss with the pesto and toasted pine nuts.

## Kid Boost
- Use red lentil or chickpea pasta for more protein, and add a side of edamame.
