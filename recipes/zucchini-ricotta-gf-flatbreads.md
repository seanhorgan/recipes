# Zucchini & Ricotta GF Flatbreads

*A light, pizza-inspired meal with natural fibers.*

## Ingredients
- 2-4 GF flatbreads
- 1.5 cups ricotta (increased for protein)
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan (added for protein boost)
- 1-2 zucchini or summer squash, sliced into rounds
- 2 cans chickpeas, mashed into the ricotta for extra protein
- olive oil, for drizzling
- red pepper flakes
- 1 handful arugula

## Sunday Prep
1. Mix the ricotta with the Parmesan and **2 cans of mashed chickpeas** for a high-protein base. Refrigerate.
2. Slice the zucchini or squash into rounds.

## Weeknight
1. **Brush or drizzle each flatbread with olive oil** before adding the spread.
2. Spread the protein-rich cheese mixture evenly over the oiled flatbreads.
3. Layer the zucchini or squash rounds on top.
4. **Generously drizzle olive oil over the entire flatbread** and the veggie rounds. Sprinkle with red pepper flakes.
5. Bake at 425°F for 12 mins.
6. Top with fresh arugula.
