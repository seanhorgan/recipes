# Shopping Rules

## Consolidation Rules
- **No Duplicates:** Merge identical or near-identical items (e.g., "Feta" and "Feta cheese"). [`ingredients.md`](ingredients.md)
  lists the names that count as the same item.
- **Quantity Merging:** Combine quantities (e.g., "Arugula (2 bags)" + "Arugula (1 bag)" = "Arugula (3 bags)").
- **Sauces:** For a linked sauce, add its ingredients to the list.
- **Grouping:** Group the list by aisle under `### Aisle` headings, in the aisle order from `ingredients.md`.
- **Pantry staples** (Kind `staple` in `ingredients.md`) stay off the list; note them under "check the pantry" instead.

## Dietary Defaults (Gluten-Free)
- **Pasta:** Default to Red Lentil or Chickpea GF pasta.
- **Bread:** Default to GF Baguettes, Sourdough, or Flatbreads.
- **Grains:** Use Quinoa, Sorghum, or Rice.

## Ingredient Specifics
How we like to buy specific items (for example, cauliflower as a 2 lb family pack of pre-cut florets, and large orange
carrots rather than baby carrots) is in the **Buy as** column of [`ingredients.md`](ingredients.md).
