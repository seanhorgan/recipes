# Honey-Mustard Citrus Glaze

## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup avocado oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, or yellow mustard
- 1 pinch salt

## Directions
1. In a jar, combine 1/3 cup avocado oil, the juice of 1 lemon (about 3 tbsp), 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, and 1 pinch salt.
2. Close the lid and shake hard for 30 seconds (or whisk vigorously) until emulsified. Store in the fridge for up to a week; shake again before using.
