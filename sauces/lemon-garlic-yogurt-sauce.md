# Lemon-Garlic Yogurt Sauce

*The kids' go-to sauce, and our stand-in wherever a recipe calls for tahini.*

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp water

## Directions
1. Stir everything together until smooth. Store in a jar in the fridge.
